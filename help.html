Common questions

How long does a transfer take?

To a Kova wallet: instantly. To an e-wallet: within 5 minutes. To a bank account: within 1 business day.

What are the fees?

A flat transfer fee of 0.50 USDC plus a 0.5% conversion fee when the money changes currency. You always see the total and the exact amount they receive before you confirm.

What is USDC?

USDC is a stablecoin: a digital token designed to stay worth 1 US dollar. Kova holds your balance in USDC so it keeps its value until you send it. You never need to buy or manage crypto yourself.

How do I add money?

Go to Add money and pick one of four ways:

  • Bank app QR: free and instant. Scan the code with your bank's app.
  • Bank transfer: free, within 1 business day.
  • Debit or credit card: 1.5% fee, instant.
  • Cash at a partner store: 1.00 USDC fee, instant once you pay.

The summary shows exactly how much USDC you'll get before you continue.

I forgot my password

In the real app you'd reset it from the sign-in page with a code sent to your mobile number. In this prototype, use the demo account: maria@kova.example with the password kova2026.

Is Kova a real product?

No. Kova Wallet is a fictional prototype made for a university class. Don't send it real money or enter real personal details.
