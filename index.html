How it works

Four steps from your paycheck to your family

Kova keeps your money as USDC, a stablecoin worth 1 US dollar, so it holds its value until you send it.

  1. Top up

    Add money by bank app QR, bank transfer, card or cash.

  2. Hold digital dollars

    Your balance is shown in USDC and in your home currency.

  3. Send

    Pick a recipient, check the fees and confirm.

  4. They cash out

    Your family gets their local currency in an e-wallet or bank.
